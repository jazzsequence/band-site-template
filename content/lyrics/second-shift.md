I come home with the hours missing
Same shoes, cleaner hands
There’s a stranger who wears my face all day
And I never shake his hand

### Pre-Chorus
You agreed to the terms
You just weren’t in the room
You said yes with a mouth
That somebody borrowed from you

### Chorus
He works the second shift
The one you’ll never see
He does the thing you couldn’t
So you could stay asleep and clean
Don’t wait up
Don’t ask where he goes
There’s a door inside your day
And a man behind it you don’t know

### Verse 2
They call it a feeling
When you can’t recall your week
They gave the ache a softer word
So you’d stop trying to speak

They said the seeing was the sickness
Said the noticing was strain
Wrote you a quieter version
And filed the rest away

### Pre-Chorus
It isn’t in your head
It’s in the paperwork
The part of you that screams
Got reassigned to someone else’s work

### Bridge
You keep asking who’s doing this to you.
Wrong question.
It’s already been done. You just weren’t home for it.
The stranger’s not the monster.
The stranger’s the receipt.

I’m not here to wake you up.
I’m just the noise that leaks under the door
of a room you didn’t know was yours.

### Breakdown / Chant
There’s a you that stays
There’s a you that goes
There’s a you that signs
There’s a you that knows

There’s a you that stays
There’s a you that goes
There’s a you they kept
And a you they chose

### Final Chorus
He works the second shift
The one you’ll never see
He carries what you buried
So the surface stays serene
Don’t wait up
Don’t learn his name
Because the day you shake his hand
Neither one of you’s the same

### Outro
They didn’t take anything.
Look — it’s all still here.
It’s just that half of it answers to a different name now,
and it’s very, very good at its job.
